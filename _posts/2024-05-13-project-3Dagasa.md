Professor Agasa's house in "Detective Conan" has always been a place I really like. Not only is it the high-tech factory for Professor Agasa and Ai Haibara, but its unique house design and interior decoration are also very impressive. I used Blender to do 3D modeling and rendering from scratch according to the "Detective Conan" setting materials. Then, by researching how to add wall thickness, proper supports, and other physical details to the model, I 3D printed it using Bambu Studio with a Bambu Lab X1 Carbon 0.4 nozzle.
